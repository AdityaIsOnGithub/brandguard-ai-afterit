## 🚀 Overview
**BrandGuard AI v3.0** is an explainable, human-in-the-loop multimodal brand-governance platform powered by **Brand Vector DNA**. It audits marketing copy and visual assets prior to publication against approved brand style guides, verified factual claims, hex color palettes, and WCAG 2.1 Level AA accessibility standards.

Instead of acting as a black-box filter that rejects or overwrites creator intent, BrandGuard AI pinpoints exact violations, cites the specific page and section of the brand book, explains why the issue was flagged, and provides non-destructive **1-Click Smart Fixes**, **Auto-Adaptive Tone Rewriting**, **Computer Vision Contrast Heatmaps**, and **Cryptographic SHA-256 Compliance Certificates**.
